_Eight stages, eight commits, one command._

You point copperhead at a markdown file and it hands back a design package. That is a
large claim for one command, and the schematic capture is the smallest part of it.
What carries it is the bookkeeping: what each stage has to prove before the next one
is allowed to start and what happens to the work when it cannot prove it.
